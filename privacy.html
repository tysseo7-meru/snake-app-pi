Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the snake game "Snake Park" (the "App") collects and how it is used. The App is a free game that runs in the Pi Browser.

Information we collect

  • Pi account information. When you sign in with Pi, the App receives your Pi username and user ID from the Pi platform. The App does not receive your passphrase or wallet keys.
  • Game data. Your best score is stored so it can appear in the shared high-score list. While you are playing, your username and current position are shared with other online players so you can see each other in the same arena.
  • On-device data. The App also stores game data in your browser's local storage as a backup.

How the data is stored

High scores are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore), a cloud database service provided by Google. Online presence is shared through Firebase Realtime Database. Data is associated with your Pi user ID and username. We do not sell your information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Payments

The App does not process Pi payments and does not collect payment information. If a Pi payment feature is added in the future, this policy will be updated first.

Data deletion

You can ask for your stored game data to be deleted by opening an issue in the App's GitHub repository (github.com/tysseo7-meru). Deletion removes your game data from the database; scores already shown to other players during play cannot be recalled.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the App's features change. The current version will always be available at this URL.
